2. What Data We Collect

We collect minimal data. Specifically:

  • Log data: GitHub Pages (our hosting provider) automatically collects standard server logs including IP address, browser type, referring page, and pages visited. We do not have direct access to these logs and do not use them to identify individuals.
  • Cookies: Our sites may use cookies to store preferences such as theme settings. These cookies are essential for site functionality and are not used for tracking.

We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers through our websites, except where you voluntarily contact us by email. All tool and calculator operations run entirely in your browser — no data you input is transmitted to any server.

  • For more information about how Google uses your data, please see How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps Where You Use Our Services.
  • You can also opt out of personalised advertising from other ad networks at Your Ad Choices or Your Online Choices (EU).

Third-party advertising companies may collect and use data about your visits to this and other websites to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

5. How We Protect Your Data

All our sites are hosted on GitHub Pages, which provides HTTPS encryption for all connections. All tools and calculators run client-side — no data you input is transmitted to our servers or any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not require registration, do not collect names or email addresses, and do not use social media plugins. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.
